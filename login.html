What we collect

Your name, email address, and the care information you and your family choose to add to Kalin.

How we use it

Only to run your family’s care hub: sign-in, sharing updates with the people you invite, and sending account emails. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Only you and the family members or caregivers you add to your hub.

Your choices

Using Kalin

Kalin helps families and care teams coordinate care. Keep your sign-in details private, and only invite people you trust.

Not medical advice

Kalin is a coordination tool. It doesn’t provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Your content

You own the information you add. You’re responsible for having permission to share information about other people.

Changes

We may update these terms and will let you know about significant changes.

How we protect your data

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS.
  • Data is encrypted at rest on our database provider.
  • Each account can only access its own family’s information.
  • Passwords are stored securely and never visible to us.

Keeping your account safe

Use a long, unique password and sign out on shared devices.

Report a problem

Found a security issue? Email security@kalin.app.

Can’t sign in?

Check your email and password, then use “Forgot password?” to get a reset link.

Didn’t get an email?

Check your spam folder, then wait a minute and try again.

Still stuck?

Email us at support@kalin.app and we’ll get back to you.
